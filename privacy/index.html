Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. What we collect

When you apply through our forms, we collect your name, email, WhatsApp number, company and store details, and your answers to the application questions. We save answers step by step, so information you enter is stored even if you don't finish the form.

2. How we use it

We use your information to review your application, contact you about our services, and deliver media buying if you become a client. We do not sell your data.

3. Where it's stored

Application data is stored in Google Sheets (Google LLC). Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log basic technical data such as your IP address.

4. Client account data

If you become a client, we access your Pinterest and store data only to run and report on your campaigns, and we keep it confidential.

5. Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store your form progress locally so you can continue where you left off.

6. How long we keep it

We keep application data for up to 24 months, or longer while you are a client, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have rights under the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Contact

For any privacy request, email us at hello@arclineads.com.
