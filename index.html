Questions we get

Short answers. Anything else, ask when you apply.

Why do you require Google and Meta to be profitable first?

Because Pinterest amplifies a store that converts; it doesn't fix one that doesn't. If Google and Meta aren't paying back, the problem is the offer, the store or the tracking — and Pinterest budget would go to the same problem. Coaching fixes that first.

What budget does the agency need?

Enough to test properly across products and to run catalog campaigns once winners are found. We set the number together in the audit, based on your AOV and margin — not on a fixed package.

Do you build the creatives?

Yes. Pins are built from what already sells in your store and what runs at your competitors, with copy written for search on Pinterest. You approve, we publish.

How is ArcLine different from a general ads agency?

We only do Pinterest, we run our own e-commerce stores on it, and we report blended ROAS — real shop revenue over all spend — instead of the platform's tracked number.

I'm a complete beginner. Is coaching for me?

Yes, that's Track 1. You need time each week and a small budget for tools and first ads. You don't need a store, a product or experience.

Can I move from coaching to the agency?

That's the intended route. As soon as Google and Meta are profitable and the tracking is in place, the agency takes over Pinterest and coaching stops.
